PFD — new attitude indicator

Panel-by-panel overhaul, top of the cockpit first. Rendered from the real Basalt code (shown at a demo pitch/roll so the ladder + tilt are visible). Colours kept; everything else on the PFD unchanged.

demo attitude ~8° nose-up, ~12° bank

What changed

  • Sky / ground fill — light blue above, brown below, split at the horizon.
  • Solid horizon line as the sky/ground border (subpixel), replacing the row of dashes.
  • Pitch ladder — a short rung every 5°, a wider rung every 10°; tilts with bank.
  • Aircraft symbol — solid yellow wings pivoting around a round centre body; the | end-caps are gone.
  • Readouts — a space added: ALT 129 Baro, SPD 145 SAS.

Pick the centre body

circle (current) · ring "( o )" · square · diamond

  • circle — a clean round dot (what's on the PFD above). My pick.
  • ring — ( o ), a wider, more prominent body.
  • square — a small solid block.
  • diamond — renders blank in the tool for now (the renderer doesn't map that glyph yet — easy to add if you want it).

Notes / open

  • The pitch rungs look slightly stair-stepped when banked (unavoidable at this resolution); at level they're clean horizontal lines.
  • The ground brown needs its palette slot set on the PFD monitor in-game (the theme repurposes the default brown slot) — a small wiring step before deploy.
  • Tunable if you want: ladder spacing/among of degrees shown, wing length/thickness, horizon line colour.
